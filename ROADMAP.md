# Roadmap

Where Quick Switch is heading after its first release. These are directions, not promises: there are no dates, and the plan can change as people use the plugin and share what they need.

- Show the switcher in the language jellyfin-web is set to.
- Optionally show Who's watching? when the app starts, turned on per device.
- Test and document Quick Switch on the official Android and iOS apps and Jellyfin Desktop.
- Keep the current page after a switch when the new account can see it.

Quick Switch stays a switcher between real Jellyfin accounts saved on a device. Profiles inside an account, PINs and server-side settings are out of scope, and so are clients that bundle their own copy of jellyfin-web, such as the Samsung Tizen store app, or native clients.

Have an idea? Share it in [Discussions → Ideas](https://github.com/pathimmersive/jellyfin-plugin-quick-switch/discussions/categories/ideas) before opening a feature request.
